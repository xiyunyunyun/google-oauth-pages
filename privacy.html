Privacy Policy

Application: vps-nai-gdrive-backup
Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how vps-nai-gdrive-backup handles Google user data when the application is used with rclone to back up files from a personal VPS server to the owner's Google Drive account.

Personal-use application. vps-nai-gdrive-backup is not a public storage service and does not provide Google Drive backup services to other users.

1. Google user data accessed

Depending on the owner's rclone configuration and the Google Drive operations being performed, the application may access:

  • Google Drive file and folder names and metadata.
  • Google Drive file contents that the owner chooses to back up, restore, synchronize, or manage.
  • Authorization information required for Google OAuth 2.0 access to the Google Drive API.

The application does not intentionally request unrelated Google services or personal information that is not needed for the configured backup workflow.

2. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the functionality requested by the owner, including uploading, downloading, listing, synchronizing, and managing files in the owner's Google Drive as configured through rclone.

The application does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or any unrelated commercial purpose.

3. How Google user data is stored

The website hosting this policy is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not receive or store the owner's Google Drive files.

When rclone is configured for Google Drive, OAuth credentials or tokens may be stored in rclone's local configuration on the owner's VPS or other environment where rclone is running. That local credential storage is controlled by the owner and is not stored by this website.

4. Sharing and transfer

vps-nai-gdrive-backup does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, information resellers, or other third parties for their independent use.

The application does not transfer Google user data for targeted advertising, personalized advertising, retargeted advertising, or credit-worthiness or lending purposes.

Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

5. AI NCII

This application does not use Google APIs to create, generate, distribute, or facilitate AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (AI NCII).

6. Third-party services

The backup workflow uses Google APIs and rclone. Google processes data under Google's applicable policies and terms. rclone is the client used by the owner to perform the configured Google Drive operations.

This privacy-policy website does not add an analytics, advertising, or tracking service of its own.

7. Data retention and deletion

This website does not maintain a server-side copy of the owner's Google Drive data. Backup copies stored in Google Drive remain there until the owner deletes them or changes the backup configuration.

The owner can revoke this application's Google account access through their Google Account settings. The owner can also remove the local rclone configuration and locally stored authorization data from the VPS.

8. Security

This application is intended for personal use. The owner is responsible for securing the VPS, rclone configuration, OAuth credentials, and any backup data stored on the VPS or in Google Drive.

9. Children's privacy

This application is not directed to children and is not intended for general public use.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's actual data practices change. The current version is published at this URL and includes the revision date above.

11. Contact
